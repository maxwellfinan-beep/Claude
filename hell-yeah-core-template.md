## 1. What Is Hell Yeah Core?

Hell yeah core is a TikTok editing aesthetic built around content that provokes an instinctive reaction of **"Hell yeah!"** It celebrates things that are viscerally, unapologetically, almost absurdly cool -- dinosaurs, Transformers, explosions, military jets, epic nature -- set to heavy 2000s rock music.

**Origin:** The "Guys Will See This and Just Think Hell Yeah" meme (Twitter, August 2023) evolved into a full TikTok editing trend by late 2024.

**The emotional formula:**

```
NOSTALGIA + SINCERITY + RAW EXCITEMENT = HELL YEAH CORE
```

- Unironic enthusiasm -- no pretense, no overthinking
- 2000s childhood nostalgia (the stuff you thought was peak cool as a kid)
- High energy, hype, adrenaline
- A defiant celebration of things you genuinely love

## 4. Video Structure Template (Beat-by-Beat)

```
TOTAL LENGTH: 15-60 seconds (TikTok sweet spot)

[0-3s]  INTRO / HOOK
        - Single bold, striking image or clip
        - Text overlay (question, bold statement, or label)
        - Must be a SCROLL-STOPPER in the first frame
        - Set to opening riff or first notes of the song

[3-12s] BUILD / VERSE
        - Clips are 1-3 seconds each, synced to rhythm
        - Slower-paced but still rhythmic
        - Introduce subtle speed ramping (slow-mo moments)
        - Build anticipation toward the drop
        - Mix in breathing room (2-3s clips) between faster cuts

[12-25s] DROP / CHORUS (THE CLIMAX)
        - MAXIMUM ENERGY
        - Clips under 1 second, sometimes just a few frames
        - Rapid-fire montage of the most intense visuals
        - Every cut aligns to a beat
        - Full velocity editing, shake, and flash effects
        - This is where dinosaurs fight robots

[25-30s] OUTRO / RESOLUTION
        - Option A: Hard cut to black
        - Option B: Final impactful image that lingers (2-3s)
        - Option C: Loop back to beginning (for replay value)
        - Optional: text overlay ("hell yeah" / creator tag)
```

### Pacing Rules
- Every cut aligns to a beat in the music
- Mix short clips (action) with slightly longer clips (breathing room)
- Slow-motion for the most epic moments, speed-ups for transitions
- The energy curve should always go UP, never plateau

## 5. Editing Techniques Reference

### Primary Tool: CapCut (Free, TikTok-integrated)

#### Velocity Editing (Speed Ramping) -- THE Signature Technique
```
How to in CapCut:
1. Select clip > Speed > Curve
2. Drag graph points to create speed variation
3. Slow-mo valleys (0.1x-0.3x) on epic moments
4. Speed peaks (5x-10x) on transitions between highlights
5. Time changes to align with beat hits
```

#### Camera Shake
```
How to in CapCut:
1. Zoom in slightly on the clip (105-110%)
2. Add keyframes on the Position property
3. Alternate position UP and DOWN on each beat hit
4. Keep shake subtle during build, aggressive during drop
```

#### Flash / White Frame Transitions
```
How to in CapCut:
1. Add a white color overlay between clips (1-3 frames)
2. OR use Overlay effects > Light transitions
3. OR Basic transition tab > Flash presets
4. Place on every major beat hit for strobe-like punctuation
```

#### Zoom Transitions
```
CapCut presets to use:
- "Zoom Shake 2"
- "Blur & Zoom"
- "Tremble Zoom"
Combine with motion blur for maximum impact.
```

#### Other Effects
- **Glitch effects:** Brief digital distortion at transitions (aggressive energy)
- **Motion blur:** Add to fast-moving sections for speed feel
- **CapCut velocity presets:** "Flash," "Retro Zoom," "Fade Blur," "Blur Shake," "Rainbow"

### Advanced Tool: After Effects

For editors who want to level up:

| Technique | How To |
|-----------|--------|
| Velocity editing | Time Warp effect OR Speed Graph with keyframe interpolation (F9 Easy Ease, adjust in Graph Editor) |

## 6. Visual Style Guide

### Color Grading
```
THE FORMULA:
1. Basic correction (exposure, white balance)
2. CRANK contrast (highlights UP, shadows DOWN, whites UP, blacks DOWN)
3. BOOST saturation (especially oranges/reds for fire/epic tones)
4. Apply consistently across ALL clips (same LUT or filter)

CapCut shortcut: "Bullet Train" filter for warm cinematic look
```

**The look:** High contrast, warm, saturated. Think Michael Bay color palette meets 2000s album cover art.

### Text Overlays
```
FONT:          Bebas Neue (first choice) or Impact
COLOR:         White
STROKE:        Black outline, 5-8px thick
CASE:          ALL CAPS
PLACEMENT:     Away from subject's eyes, avoid edges where TikTok UI overlaps
LENGTH:        5-8 words per burst MAX
ANIMATION:     Appear on beat drops, words appear sequentially for emphasis
```

**When to use text:**
- First 1-3 seconds as the hook (question or bold statement)
- Throughout to label clips or add emphasis ("HELL YEAH")
- Animated pop-ups on beat drops

### Optional 2000s Aesthetic Touches
- CRT TV scan line textures
- Flame graphics
- Tribal patterns
- Low-res stylization overlays
- VHS/glitch overlays

## 9. Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Content Mistakes
| Mistake | Why It Kills Your Edit |
|---------|----------------------|
| Weak images / filler clips | One mediocre clip breaks the spell -- people swipe away |
| Poor image/clip quality | Blurry or low-res immediately kills engagement |
| Wrong music choice | Audio that doesn't match the energy or era feels inauthentic |
| Over-editing | Too many filters/transitions = chaotic. TikTok penalizes over-edited content |
| No thematic coherence | Random clips stitched together lose narrative momentum |
| Being ironic when it should be sincere | The trend works because it's GENUINELY enthusiastic. Irony undermines it |

### Technical Mistakes
| Mistake | Why It Kills Your Edit |
|---------|----------------------|
| No captions/text overlays | Hurts reach (many users scroll muted) |
| Bad cover image / weak first frame | No one engages regardless of what follows |
| Editing entirely outside TikTok | Algorithm favors in-app tools (text, captions, native music) |
| Wrong posting times | Missing peak engagement windows tanks initial reach |
| One-sided audio | Audio from only one channel sounds broken |
| Too many slides (>10) or too few (<5) | Sweet spot is 5-10 |

## 10. Quick-Start Workflow Checklist

Use this every time you make a new edit:

### Pre-Production (5-10 min)
- [ ] **Pick your theme** (dinosaurs? Transformers? military? nature? mix?)
- [ ] **Choose your song** (Brain Stew is the safe bet; check trending audio for bonus)
- [ ] **Collect 5-10 clips/images** that each independently scream "hell yeah"
- [ ] **Arrange by energy level** (moderate openers, intense climax clips)

### Production (15-30 min)
- [ ] **Open CapCut** > New Project > 9:16 > 60fps
- [ ] **Import audio track** and identify: intro beats, build section, DROP, outro
- [ ] **Place your hook clip** (0-3s) -- the single most striking visual
- [ ] **Add hook text overlay** (bold statement, Bebas Neue, white + black stroke)
- [ ] **Lay out build section clips** (1-3s each, synced to rhythm)
- [ ] **Lay out drop section clips** (sub-1s, rapid-fire, your most intense visuals)
- [ ] **Add velocity editing** (Curve speed: slow-mo on epic moments, speed-up on transitions)
- [ ] **Add camera shake** on beat hits (keyframed position)
- [ ] **Add white flash transitions** between clips on major beats (1-3 frames)
- [ ] **Add zoom transitions** where appropriate (Zoom Shake 2 or Blur & Zoom)
- [ ] **Apply color grading** (high contrast, warm saturation, consistent across all clips)
- [ ] **Add any additional text pop-ups** on beat drops
- [ ] **Set outro** (hard cut to black OR lingering final image OR loop)

### Post-Production (5 min)
- [ ] **Watch the full edit 3 times** -- does every second hit? Cut anything that doesn't
- [ ] **Check the first frame** -- is it an instant scroll-stopper?
- [ ] **Export:** 1080x1920, 60fps, H.264, 10+ Mbps, Smart HDR ON, +30 sharpness
- [ ] **Re-watch the export** for compression artifacts

### Publishing (2 min)
- [ ] **Upload to TikTok** during peak window (Tue-Thu 2-6 PM or Sun 9 AM)
- [ ] **Write caption** with "hell yeah core" in first 150 characters
- [ ] **Add 3-5 hashtags:** `#hellyeahcore #hellyeah #fyp` + 1-2 content-specific
- [ ] **Use TikTok's in-app text/captions** if possible (algorithmic boost)
- [ ] **Set cover image** to the most striking frame
- [ ] **Post and engage** with comments in the first 3 hours
